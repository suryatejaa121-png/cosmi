Breaking difficult news — extraction

A 29-year-old is told their lower-right molar cannot be saved and needs extraction. Assess how the candidate delivers the news and responds to the patient’s concerns.

Task

Explain the diagnosis and the recommended treatment, check understanding, and agree the next step.

Candidate submission — transcript

Candidate: Thanks for coming in. I’m afraid I’ve got some news about that back tooth that isn’t what we’d hoped.

Patient: Oh… go on.

Candidate: So we’ll need to take the tooth out because it’s got an apical abscess and it’s non-vital, okay?

Patient: Right… take it out. Is there nothing else you can do?

Candidate: Not really, no. Take some ibuprofen and we’ll sort it at your next appointment.
