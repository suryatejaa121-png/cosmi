The challenge

Operations ran on
WhatsApp, spreadsheets and paper.

ReSrishti runs a zero-landfill facility with nine recycling technologies under one roof, serving industrial, hospital, FMCG and corporate clients across Telangana. Day-to-day operations lived in spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and hand-stitched PDFs — slow for clients who need audit-ready paperwork for ESG reporting.

Before

Pickup requests arrived on WhatsApp and were scheduled by hand
Field attendance was hard to verify from the office
Certificates of Disposal were typed in Word, signed and scanned
Client records lived in spreadsheets and PDF threads

With Cosmiron

Every pickup tracked from request to weighed, processed and certified
Geofenced, selfie-verified check-ins with a GPS trail
Certificates generated as branded PDFs from recorded weights
Clients self-serve pickups, certificates and impact in their own portal
Why Cosmiron

They wanted one partner who could turn a facility run on WhatsApp, spreadsheets and paper into a single system — public website, workforce app and client portal — and keep building on it as operations grew.

Inside the platform

Attendance you can trust,
even without a signal.

Field teams work from trucks and plant floors with patchy connectivity. Check-ins are geofenced, require a live selfie, and queue offline with background sync, replaying when the phone reconnects — so managers approve attendance against location and photo evidence instead of taking it on trust.

  • Books and tracks waste pickups from request to certificate
  • Verifies field attendance with geofence, live selfie and GPS trail
  • Pushes real-time updates by web push, in-app sockets and email
  • Generates branded Certificates of Disposal as downloadable PDFs
  • Gives clients a self-service portal for pickups, certificates and impact
  • Works offline as an installable app on Android, iOS and desktop
